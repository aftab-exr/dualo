## Rules of Execution

1. **Visible Grid Lines:** Every card, container, and section uses clean 1px structural borders (`border-black` or `border-zinc-200`).
2. **Swiss Red (#ff3300) Focal Point:** Reserved strictly for primary CTAs, active match badges, and key interactive states.
3. **Asymmetric Composition:** Avoid balanced 3-column layouts. Use bold typographic scale contrasts (large Archivo headlines paired with JetBrains Mono metadata).
4. **No Gradients or Heavy Shadows:** Flat colors only.
